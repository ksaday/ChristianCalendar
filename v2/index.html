“There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God.”— Hebrews 4:9

Method: continuous Julian Day Number counting in arbitrary-precision integers — Sabbath #n = JDN 1,733,205 + 7×(n−1). The seven-day week ran unbroken through the 1582 reform. Sabbath numbers before #1 are a mathematical projection of the cycle and make no claim about observance. Calendar labels carry their own certainty: proleptic Gregorian and Julian dates, calculated Hebrew dates before 922 CE, and Chinese dates before 1929 are conventions or reconstructions, never asserted as observed dates.

Bible-event dates are traditions or estimates (Ussher, Seder Olam, early-date conservative, critical/historical), each with its source; they are projected onto the unbroken cycle and never feed it.
